## Key Decisions

- Use incremental migration, not a big-bang rewrite.
- Prioritize parity and consolidation before purity.
- View-to-Compose is now functionally complete: `ComposeMainActivity` is the sole Activity, the legacy Activity/dialog/panel/adapter/base stack is deleted, and `EditorScreen` renders preview through Compose `Canvas`.
- Do not reintroduce a `ViewInfo` or `AndroidView`-bridged renderer contract. `WaterMarkImageView` and `ViewInfo` are deleted; new rendering work goes through `WatermarkRenderer` / `:shared` commonMain.
- `WatermarkGeometry` is already commonMain and drives both preview and export cell sizing. The remaining C2 work is the cell composition/drawing rewrite into commonMain Compose graphics — **for Desktop/iOS** (`composeOverBackground`, done); **Android composition was decided NO-GO (S4d-190)** and stays native (`WatermarkRenderer.compose`).
- `MainViewModel` is not a current shared-business/IO implementation lane (S4d-191). Shared editor use-cases with real consumers are already commonMain; the rest is Android UI or Android IO/render.
- iOS renderer tests now have a coarse perceptual/stability layer for both cells and composition (S4d-192/S4d-193); keep it host-font-robust and do not turn it into byte-exact or bundled-font packaging proof.
- Desktop/iOS shared renderer config mappings now live once in commonMain (S4d-195). Android remains on its native `Paint.Style`/`StaticLayout` edge and is not a consumer of these Compose-side helpers.
- Keep Android shippable at every step. Renderer changes require goldens plus visual screenshot inspection, not byte-size inference.
- Use project files as the shared source of truth for planning and iteration.
- ACSP/cowork is historical only as of 2026-07-03. Current work follows `codex-goal.md`: direct Codex implementation, verification, local commit, and durable doc update per slice.

- S4d-1 proved commonMain Compose cannot be introduced safely until `:app` and `:shared` share one coherent Compose lineage. - **Icon raster — Android stays native (S4d-8 Option A, 2026-06-18):** the Android icon production/export draw-swap to commonMain `composeIconCell` is **abandoned**. S4d-6/S4d-7 proved commonMain Compose cannot byte-match `android.graphics.Canvas.drawBitmap` for rotated non-uniform icons (no `drawImage` overload gives float-placement + nearest-filter together; delta floored at 5/1936 px on the default-315° golden). Android keeps `WatermarkRenderer.buildIconShader`; `composeIconCell` (with the S4d-7 float improvement) is the **Desktop/iOS** icon renderer; the shared win is **geometry** (`WatermarkGeometry`), not a byte-identical Android raster. Reopening needs an owner decision (tolerance golden policy or commonMain nearest blitter). See ADR-0004 addendum.
- **Text raster — Android multiline bug fixed natively (S4d-14C, 2026-06-18):** Android keeps native `WatermarkRenderer.buildTextShader`, but the multiline vertical offset now uses full `StaticLayout.height` instead of only line 0. This removes bottom clipping and converges Android's multiline placement policy with commonMain. It is **not** a text draw-swap; bundled-font and on-device parity remain prerequisites before replacing Android text rastering with `composeTextCell`.

- **Bundled font env — accepted test-only (S4d-16, 2026-06-18):** exact Noto Sans Latin + Noto Sans CJK SC regular artifacts are now test-only resources behind `TextRasterEnv` on desktopTest and androidTest.
